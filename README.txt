SUNNY DOHTY THE BARISTA - static site

1. Open this folder in VS Code.
2. Add photos/videos to assets/gallery/ and list them in config.js (GALLERY).
3. Add social links and email in config.js (SITE).
4. Deploy: drag this whole folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop
   Contact form submissions appear in Netlify > Forms.
Likes, comments and the Studio are not part of this version.

Social previews: paste public post links into SOCIAL in config.js. They show as a scrolling row under the gallery.
