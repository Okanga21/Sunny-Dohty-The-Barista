About Sunny Dohty

The person behind the cup.

I'm Derrick, a professional barista and beverage creator passionate about transforming everyday drinks into extraordinary experiences.

From beautifully crafted specialty coffee and artistic latte designs to refreshing smoothies, signature milkshakes and innovative beverages, my mission is to inspire, create and elevate the beverage experience.

My vision

To inspire the next generation of coffee lovers and beverage professionals while helping businesses discover the creativity and possibilities behind every cup.
